Pancakes
Ingredients: 1 cup of flour, 1 large egg, 1 cup of milk
Instructions: 1. Mix ingredients into large bowl. 2. Heat a skillet at medium-low temperature. 3. Pour batter and cook until golden. 4. Enjoy!!

Omelette
Ingredients: 2 large eggs, Salt (As desired), Vegetables (As desired)
Instructions: 1. Beat eggs and combine with salt as desired. 2. Add your choice of veggies. 3. Cook in pan until set. 4. Enjoy!!

Breakfast Potatoes
Ingredients: Potatoes, Oil (Olive, Canola, or personal preference), Seasoning (As desired), Cheese (Optional)
Instructions: 1. Dice potatoes. 2. Fry in oil until crispy and golden. 3. Season and sprinkle your choice of cheese on top. 4. Enjoy!!

Japanese Onigiri
Ingredients: Cooked rice (Jasmine or personal preference), Nori (Seaweed), Filling (e.g. pickled plum, salmon, or personal preference)
Instructions: 1. Shape the rice into a triangle. 2. Insert chosen filling as desired. 3. Wrap the rice triangle with nori. 4. Enjoy!!

Chinese Shaobing
Ingredients: 1 Cup of Flour, 4 Tablespoons of Sesame seeds, Vegetable Oil
Instructions: 1. Make dough and flatten it. 2. Coat the top of the dough with sesame seeds. 3. Bake the dough until golden. 4. Enjoy!!
